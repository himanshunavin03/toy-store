5. In Dashboard, attach final product images to each of the **existing 14 Wix product IDs** listed in the project context. Use Wix Media and update the actual products; do not embed decorative repository images as substitute product photos. Dashboard edits should then flow to native galleries and Product Page.

7. Search requires inspection of the installed search widget/app. Do not write a custom search against a stale product list. Account, wishlist, cart, checkout and order-history widgets likewise require Preview checks rather than source-level assumptions.

- Check Preview at **1280px and wider**, tablet around 768–1024px, and mobile around 375–430px. At 1280px all eight top-level menu labels must be visible. Check no clipped dropdowns, readable hero/CTA, category cards, 4/2/1 product columns, banner crop, animation poster, accordion, and footer stacks.
